A curated collection of the 20 best YouTube videos about Google Antigravity and the Antigravity CLI, published as a single static page. Like its sibling the [Hermes Agent Playlist](https://github.com/avnit/hermes-agent-playlist), the curation was done by Hermes Agent rather than by hand.

## The concepts

Antigravity has several surfaces — the desktop app, the CLI, the IDE — and the YouTube coverage is scattered across all of them. The playlist puts the videos in an order that makes sense for someone starting from zero:

- Introduction & Basics (4 videos)
- Full Tutorials & Courses (4 videos)
- Antigravity CLI Specific (5 videos)
- Comparisons & Keynotes (5 videos)
- Advanced Workflows & Developer Guide (2 videos)

The 20 videos add up to more than 5.8M views as of August 2026.

## How it's built

One `index.html`, served by GitHub Pages. No framework, no build pipeline.

The playlist also became research input: watching what these 20 videos cover made it obvious what they *don't* cover, which turned into the [Antigravity Hidden Features](https://github.com/avnit/antigravity-hidden-features) video package.

The live page is at [avnit.github.io/antigravity-playlist](https://avnit.github.io/antigravity-playlist/).
